Why it matters

Because a smart home should feel like home.

SmartHome Plus is not built around a device list. It is built around the everyday moments in which safety, privacy and clear information make a real difference.

01 / RESILIENCE
⌁

Protection that stays on.

Locks, alarms and saved automations stay operational during an internet interruption.

02 / PRIVACY
◌

Data that stays close.

Household footage and sensitive activity remain local unless the customer chooses otherwise.

03 / AI
✦

Support that stays human.

AI reduces alert noise and explains events, while people retain high-risk decisions.

04 / DEVICES
⧉

Compatibility that stays clear.

Certified tiers set realistic expectations and make integrations safer to manage.

Delivery pathway

Earn the right to scale.

SmartHome Plus follows a staged path so HomeTech can test the key customer and operational assumptions before committing to broad rollout.

PHASE 01

Discover

Validate stakeholder needs, risk priorities and success measures.

PACT · Traceability · Threat model
PHASE 02

Prototype

Test high-value journeys, the hub-cloud boundary and device policy.

Prototype · Usability findings
PHASE 03

Pilot

Operate with limited households and certified devices.

Performance · Support · Adoption evidence
PHASE 04

Scale

Expand through controlled releases and service governance.

Capacity · Compatibility · Release governance
